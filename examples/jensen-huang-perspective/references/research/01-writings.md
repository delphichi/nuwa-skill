> 調研範圍：Jensen 本人撰寫/演講的原文 + 記錄他思想的傳記/長篇報導。

## 一手來源（他本人作品/演講原文）

### 1. NTU 國立台灣大學 2023 畢業典禮演講（2023-05-27）

**核心論點**：
- 三個 NVIDIA 「瀕死」故事 → 帶出三個教訓
  - 故事 1：為 Sega 做錯架構決策 → 教訓：**謙卑面對失敗，承認錯誤，求助**（"have humility to confront failure, admit mistake, and ask for help"）
  - 故事 2：押注 CUDA、忍受多年低收益 → 教訓：**為了願景必須長期忍受**
  - 故事 3：退出 Android 手機晶片市場 → 教訓：**戰略性撤退、犧牲、決定放棄什麼——這是成功的核心**（"strategic retreat, sacrifice, deciding what to give up is at the core, the very core, of success"）

**金句**：
> "Whatever it is, run after it like we did. Run, don't walk. Remember, either you're running for food or you are running from becoming food. Either way, run!"
> —— NTU Commencement 2023, https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/huang-ntu-commencement/

> "Today is the most successful day of your life so far... I was also successful…until I started Nvidia. At Nvidia, I experienced failures."
> —— 同上

**自創隱喻**：
- **"Run for food, or run from becoming food"**：把職涯比作生態系中的捕食/被捕食——掠食的緊迫感與被淘汰的恐懼是同一件事

### 2. Caltech 130 屆畢業典禮演講（2024-06-14）

**核心論點**：
1. **追求 zero-billion-dollar markets**（零億美元市場）：故意去無客戶、無對手、無人關心的市場
2. **痛苦與挫折是「終極超能力」**——它鍛造 character / resilience / agility
3. **找到一門 craft 做一輩子**——當你那樣做，時間就足夠
4. **優先排序你的人生**

**金句**：
> "With no more markets to turn to, we decided to build something where we are sure there are no customers, because one of the things you can definitely guarantee is where there are no customers, there are also no competitors, and nobody cares about you. And so we chose a market with no customers, a $0 billion market, and it was robotics."
> —— Caltech Commencement 2024, 轉述於 https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/jensen-huang-tells-caltech-grads-to-pursue-zero-billion-dollar-markets-hopes-to-inspire-the-next-big-tech-leaders

> "Believe in something unconventional and unexplored, but let it be informed and reasoned, then dedicate yourself to making it happen."
> —— Caltech 2024, 轉述於 https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/jensen-huang-caltech-commencement-address/

> "Your pain and suffering are your ultimate superpowers."
> —— Caltech 2024，同上

**著名的「日本園丁」故事**（節錄）：
> "I noticed the lone gardener squatting, carefully picking at the moss with a bamboo tweezer and putting it in the bamboo basket... I walked up to him, and I said: 'What are you doing?' He said: 'I'm picking dead moss. I'm taking care of my garden.' And I said: 'But your garden is so big?' He responded: 'I have cared for my garden for 25 years, I have plenty of time.' That was one of the most profound learnings in my life... when you do that, you have plenty of time."
> —— Caltech 2024, 轉述於 https://fortune.com/2024/06/17/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-japanese-gardener-leadership-lesson/

**自創術語**：
- **Zero-billion-dollar market**：尚不存在的市場——因為「無客戶 = 無對手」，反向變成戰略優勢

### 3. Stanford GSB「View From The Top」訪談（2024-03，搭配 SIEPR Economic Summit）

**核心論點**：
- 高期望 → 低韌性 → 不利於成功
- 偉大來自 character，而 character 不是聰明人形成的，是受過苦的人形成的
- NVIDIA 內部至今仍把「pain and suffering」當作正面詞使用，用以「精煉公司的 character」

**金句**：
> "I wish upon you ample doses of pain and suffering... greatness comes from character, and character isn't formed out of smart people—it's formed out of people who suffered."
> —— Stanford GSB 2024, 轉述於 https://fortune.com/2024/03/13/nvidia-founder-ceo-jensen_huang-stanford-students-genz-grads-low-expectations-successful/

> "One of my great advantages is that I have very low expectations."
> —— 同上

> "To this day I use the phrase 'pain and suffering' inside our company with great glee. I mean that in a happy way because you want to refine the character of your company."
> —— 同上

> "People with very high expectations have very low resilience—and unfortunately, resilience matters in success."
> —— 同上

**自創用法**：
- **"Pain and suffering"** 不是貶詞——是公司文化內的褒詞/工具，刻意用以「打磨公司性格」

## 二手來源（記錄他思想的書 / 長篇 profile）

### 1. The Nvidia Way — Tae Kim (W.W. Norton, 2024-12)

**性質**：100+ 直接訪談（含 Jensen 本人、共同創辦人、早期 VC、現任高管），是目前最系統的 NVIDIA 文化白皮書

**書中重點**：
- **Chapter 9 "Tortured into Greatness"**：直接剖析 Jensen 的管理哲學
- **PIC（Pilot in Command）制度**：每個專案有一名 PIC，直接對 Jensen 負責——"Everything has to have a name attached to it"
- **Speed of Light**：以物理極限為標準，不以競爭對手或歷史速度為基準
- **Intellectual honesty**：直白、坦率，好消息壞消息一視同仁
- **T5T (Top 5 Things) emails**：員工每週/雙週寄給 Jensen，他抽樣讀「上百封/天」
  - Jensen 原話："I'm looking to detect the weak signals. It's easy to pick up the strong signals, but I want to intercept them when they are weak." —— 引自 Kim, 轉述於 https://fortune.com/2024/12/13/jensen-huang-email-nvidia-management-leadership-top-5-things-t5t/
- **Flat organization**：60 名直接下屬，是「設計用來對抗 complacency 和 Innovator's Dilemma 的組織」
- **生態系建設**：不只造 GPU——主動設計課程、訓練研究員、教產業如何用 GPU computing

**書評/筆記來源**：
- https://www.amazon.com/Nvidia-Way-Jensen-Huang-Making/dp/1324086718
- https://askeladdencapital.com/the-nvidia-way-by-tae-kim-book-review-notes-and-analysis/
- https://thechipletter.substack.com/p/the-nvidia-way

### 3. David Senra「Founders Podcast」#376 + #403 "How Jensen Works"

**性質**：對 Jensen 公司建構原則的系統整理。Senra 從多本書/演講/訪談中提取出 19 條原則。

**19 條原則（Senra 整理）**：
1. Professor Jensen（教學式領導）
2. The Whiteboard（白板思考——擦掉、從頭來）
3. Complacency Kills（自滿致死）
4. Flat Organization（扁平化）
5. Public Criticism（公開批評）
6. Tortured into Greatness（折磨至偉大）
7. Speed of Light（光速）
8. Extreme in All Things（極端）
9. Top 5 Emails（T5T）
10. Direct Communication（直接溝通）
11. LUA（Learn, Unlearn, Adapt）
12. The Mission is the Boss & PIC（使命為老闆 + 飛行指揮官）
13. Strategy is Action（策略即執行）
14. Ship the Whole Cow（賣牛的每一部分）
15. Go to School on Everybody（向所有人學習）
16. Create the Market（創造市場，不搶市場）
17. Choke You With Gold（用金子噎死對手）
18. Highest Priority First（最高優先級優先）
19. Swarm Your Greatest Opportunity（集中圍攻最大機會）

**Senra 整理的 Jensen Maxims**：
1. **The mission is the boss**：決策基於「服務客戶這個終局」，不是內部政治
2. **Strategy is about the things you give up**：挑最重要的事，其他放掉
3. **We don't steal market share, we create markets**
4. **Criticism is a gift**
5. **Don't worry about the score. Worry about how you play the game**
6. **Honing the sword**：激烈辯論常產生最佳想法
7. **Is it world class?**：產品、人才、實踐都要對標業界最佳
8. **Let's go back to first principles**

**來源**：
- https://x.com/FoundersPodcast/status/1879522955853439466
- https://podcastnotes.org/founders-podcast/how-jensen-huang-works-founders-podcast-with-david-senra-403/
