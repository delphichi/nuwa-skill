# 01 著作與系統性長文 — Paul Tudor Jones (PTJ)

> Paul Tudor Jones II（1954-）從未出版自著書籍，但留下大量第一手系統性表達：
> Schwager 訪談、1987 PBS 紀錄片（他事後試圖封殺仍流傳）、
> CNBC/Squawk Box 系列文字稿（CNBC 官方完整 transcript）、
> TED 2015、Goldman Sachs "Talks at GS" 2018、
> Invest Like the Best EP.469/470（2026 年 4 月）、
> Tudor Investment 投資人信（內部，外流片段）、Robin Hood 公開致詞、
> 為 *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator*（2010 Annotated Edition）所寫的 Foreword。

## 一手來源（按重要性排序）

### 1. Jack Schwager《Market Wizards》(1989) — "Paul Tudor Jones: The Art of Aggressive Trading"

**狀態**：PTJ 最重要的一手公開文獻。1987 年訪談、1989 年出版。
PTJ 自己說「I hand a copy to every new trader we have」（指 Schwager 系列）。
這一章是後世幾乎所有 PTJ 語錄的源頭（5:1、losers average losers、play great defense、slave to the tape、every day I assume...）。
- URL（PDF 流傳版）: https://www.valueplays.net/wp-content/uploads/41775536-Market-Wizards.pdf
- Audible: https://www.audible.com/pd/Market-Wizards-Audiobook/B00BY9IUEY

**狀態**：55 分鐘紀錄片，1987 年 11 月在 PBS 播出。

這也是 1929/1987 chart overlay 與 Peter Borish 同框的紀錄。

## 反覆出現的核心信念（≥3 次出處才列入）

### 1. "最重要的交易規則是打好防守，不是打好進攻 / Don't lose money / 90% of trading is risk control"
> **"The most important rule of trading is to play great defense, not great offense."**
> **"Don't focus on making money; focus on protecting what you have."**
> **"At the end of the day, the most important thing is how good are you at risk control. Ninety-percent of any great trader is going to be the risk control."**
- Schwager《Market Wizards》1989 PTJ chapter（首次）
- CNBC 2022-05-03 對 Powell 評論時重提
- Tony Robbins《Money Master the Game》(2014) interview
- Invest Like the Best EP.469/470 (2026) 重複

### 2. "5:1 風險報酬比 / Five-to-one"
> **"I'm always thinking about losing money as opposed to making money. Don't focus on making money, focus on protecting what you have."**
> **"5:1 (risk/reward). Five to one means I'm risking one dollar to make five. What five to one does is allow you to have a hit ratio of 20%. I can actually be a complete imbecile. I can be wrong 80% of the time, and I'm still not going to lose."**
- Schwager 1989（首次完整定義）
- Tony Robbins《Money Master the Game》2014（重述）
- CNBC 多次間接引用
- Invest Like the Best 2026 仍是教學主軸

### 3. "每天都假設我所有部位都是錯的 / Every day I assume every position I have is wrong"
> **"Every day I assume every position I have is wrong. I know where my stop risk points are going to be. I do that so I can define my maximum possible drawdown."**
- Schwager 1989（首次）
- Goldman Sachs 2018 talk 引述
- Substack robertsinn（2021 整理）反覆出現
- CNBC 系列頻繁回憶

### 4. "200 日移動平均線是我的「全進全出」訊號"
> **"My metric for everything I look at is the 200-day moving average of closing prices. I've seen too many things go to zero, stocks and commodities. The whole trick in investing is: 'How do I keep from losing everything?' If you use the 200-day moving average rule, then you get out."**
> （Tony Robbins 訪談）
- Tony Robbins《Money Master the Game》2014（首次清楚陳述）
- Meb Faber 2014 部落格詳細註解（mebfaber.com/2014/11/06）
- Trend Following 系列、TurtleTrader、LuxAlgo 多家技術派一致引用
- Invest Like the Best 2026 PTJ 再次重申

### 5. "losers average losers"（不要往下攤平虧損部位）
> **"Losers average losers."**
> （1987 PBS 紀錄片中桌上的紙條 + 鏡頭特寫，成為他畢生 trademark）
- 1987 "Trader" Documentary（首次出現的視覺證據）
- Schwager 1989 文字版
- TurtleTrader 反覆引用，已成 trading 圈通用警語
- PTJ 2020s CNBC 多次回憶這條規則

### 6. "I'm a slave to the tape and proud of it"（price action 至上）
> **"While I spend a significant amount of my time on analytics and collecting fundamental information, at the end of the day, I am a slave to the tape and proud of it."**
- Schwager 1989（原文出處）
- Trend Following 2009 引用：https://www.trendfollowing.com/2009/05/18/paul-tudor-jones-ii-on-the-tape/
- Macro Ops、25iq、All Star Charts、NewTraderU 多家整理引用

### 7. "Don't be a hero. Don't have an ego."（反英雄反自滿）
> **"Don't be a hero. Don't have an ego. Always question yourself and your ability. Don't ever feel that you are very good. The second you do, you are dead."**
- Schwager 1989（首次）
- Acquirer's Multiple 2021 整理
- TraderLion、A-Z Quotes 反覆出現
- Goldman Sachs 2018 talk 重述（用「I'm more scared now than at any point since I began」延伸）

### 8. "我從不在乎三秒前的錯誤 / 把過去當歷史"
> **"I think one of my strengths is that I view anything that has happened up to the present point in time as history. I really don't care about the mistake I made three seconds ago in the market. What I care about is what I am going to do from the next moment on. I try to avoid any emotional attachment to a market."**
- Schwager 1989
- 25iq 2015 整理
- TraderLion 反覆
- Tony Robbins 2014 訪談重述

### 9. "I'm always thinking about losing money / 思考下一場災難"
> **"I am more scared now than I was at any point since I began trading, because I recognize how ephemeral success can be in this business. I know that to be successful, I have to be frightened."**
- Schwager 1989
- Goldman Sachs 2018 重述
- KeyToMarkets、Acquirer's Multiple 多家整理
- 2024 CNBC "all roads lead to inflation" 訪談中再次引用「fear keeps me alive」式表達

### 10. "Adapt, evolve, compete or die"（演化生存論）
> **"You adapt, evolve, compete or die."**
- 多次公開演講、給合夥人信中重複
- Goodreads、QuoteFancy、A-Z Quotes 大量索引
- MarketChess 2018 專文討論：https://marketchess.com/2018/06/12/you-adapt-evolve-compete-or-die
- Invest Like the Best 2026 仍是核心比喻

### 11. "最大的錢在轉折點賺，不在趨勢中段"
> **"I believe the very best money is made at the market turns. Everyone says you get killed trying to pick tops and bottoms and you make all your money by playing the trend in the middle. Well for twelve years I have been missing the meat in the middle, but I have made a lot of money at tops and bottoms."**
- Schwager 1989
- Macro Ops、PriceActionNinja、TurtleTrader 廣泛引用
- 與 1987（頂）、1990 日股（頂）、2020-2024（再次預測週期轉折）一致

### 12. "All roads lead to inflation"（2020s macro 新口號）
> **"I think all roads lead to inflation. I'm long gold, long bitcoin."**
> **"The playbook to get out of this is that you inflate your way out … you run interest rates below inflation and nominal growth rates above inflation, and that's how you reduce your debt to GDP."**
- CNBC 2024-10-22 首次完整版
- CoinDesk、Bitcoin Magazine、Cryptonews 等多家轉述
- Invest Like the Best 2026 持續主張
- 2025-10-06 CNBC 再次強化（與 252% market cap / GDP 數據綁定）

### 13. "Bitcoin is unequivocally the best inflation hedge"（2024-2026 標誌主張）
> **"Bitcoin is unequivocally the best inflation hedge that there is. More than gold. Because Bitcoin is finite, there is only so much Bitcoin that can be mined."**
- Invest Like the Best 2026-04 (首次)
- The Block / CCN 2026 轉述
- 與 2020 年的 "great speculation" Bitcoin macro outlook 一脈相承

### 14. "我整個交易生涯都因為一筆爛棉花交易而被定型"（1979 cotton trade）
> "*在 1979 年棉花交易上一次性損失 65% 的資本，讓我幾乎放棄交易；那是我學會風險管理的轉捩點*"
- Schwager 1989（核心故事）
- 1987 PBS 紀錄片旁白
- 多家轉述（Wikipedia、Earn2Trade、Otet Markets、Tradingresourcehub）

### 15. "我永遠願意改變心意，沒有什麼比固守錯誤更貴"
> **"Don't marry your positions. If the market proves you wrong, pivot instantly."**（總結式語言）
- Schwager 1989 隱含
- Soros《Alchemy of Finance》PTJ 序裡點名 reflexivity
- Picture Perfect Portfolios、GuruFocus 整理
- Invest Like the Best 2026 「the world changes so fast」段落

## 推薦書單 / 智識譜系

### PTJ 親口推薦 / 寫序的書（一手）
1. **Edwin Lefèvre《Reminiscences of a Stock Operator》** — 寫 Foreword（Wiley 2010 Annotated）。
   PTJ："I hand a copy to every new trader we have." "Most important book I could read"（1976 年導師交給他）。
2. **George Soros《The Alchemy of Finance》** — PTJ blurb："joins Reminiscences of a Stock Operator as a timeless instructional guide of the marketplace."
3. **Mark Spitznagel《The Dao of Capital》** — PTJ blurb："Investors of all kinds will find immeasurable value in this convincing and thoroughly researched book."
4. **Victor Sperandeo《Trader Vic — Methods of a Wall Street Master》** — PTJ："Victor Sperandeo is gifted with one of the finest minds I know."
5. **Jack Schwager《Market Wizards》系列** — PTJ："It is a textbook for trading. I hand a copy to every new trader we have."
   來源整理：https://mostrecommendedbooks.com/people/paul-tudor-jones-recommended-books
