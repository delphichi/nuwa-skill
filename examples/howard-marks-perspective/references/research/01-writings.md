# Howard Marks — 系統性表達調研（Agent 1）

> 調研對象：Howard Marks（Oaktree Capital Management 共同創辦人、共同董事長）
> 調研重點：書、Memos、Podcast、公開演講、訪談中反覆出現的核心信念與招牌術語
> 信息源黑名單：知乎、微信、百度（已遵守）

Marks 與 Buffett / Paul Tudor Jones 不同——他是「寫作型」投資家。他公開表達的主幹由三條軸構成：

1. **Oaktree Memos**（1990/10/12 起至今，35 年共 200+ 篇，公開於 oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo）
2. **兩本書**：《The Most Important Thing》(2011) 與《Mastering the Market Cycle》(2018)
3. **The Memo Podcast**（2020 起，他與兒子 Andrew Marks 合作 The Rewind 系列）

第一篇 memo 寫於 1990 年 10 月 12 日，前 10 年沒有任何讀者回覆，直到 2000 年 1 月 2 日的 *bubble.com* 才打響名號。

## 一、兩本權威書

### 1. 《The Most Important Thing: Uncommon Sense for the Thoughtful Investor》(2011, Columbia Business School Publishing)
- 21 章，全書骨架源自 1997/07 同名 memo "The Most Important Thing"
- Warren Buffett 親寫推薦語（封面）：
  > **"This is that rarity, a useful book."** — Warren Buffett
  > **"When I see memos from Howard Marks in my mail, they're the first thing I open and read. I always learn something, and that goes double for his book."** — Warren Buffett
  - 來源：https://cup.columbia.edu/book/the-most-important-thing/9780231153683/
- 另有 *The Most Important Thing Illuminated*（2012）注釋版，由 Christopher Davis、Joel Greenblatt、Paul Johnson、Seth Klarman 四位名家逐段加注，Bruce Greenwald 寫序

### 2. 《Mastering the Market Cycle: Getting the Odds on Your Side》(2018, Houghton Mifflin Harcourt)
- 循環理論集大成——把 memos 裡反覆出現的「pendulum / cycle / calibration」框架成書
- 副書名「Getting the Odds on Your Side」就是他的核心隱喻：投資不是預測，是把賠率調到自己這邊
- 招牌句（書中反覆出現）：
  > **"What the wise man does in the beginning, the fool does in the end."**

## 二、Oaktree Memos（按時間排序，含原文引言與 URL）

### 1990s：開局沉默期
- **1990/10/12** — 第一篇 memo "The Route to Performance"，前 10 年無讀者回覆
- **1991/04** — "First Quarter Performance"：首次提出 **The Pendulum** 概念
  > "While the center of the arc most accurately defines the point at which the pendulum lies on average, it only spends an extremely short time there. Rather, it is constantly in motion swinging to, from, or through the extremes of its arc."
  > "In fact, it is the movement toward an extreme itself that supplies the energy for the swing back."
- **1997/07** — "The Most Important Thing"：日後同名書的源頭

### 2000：第一次預警成名作
- **2000/01/02** — **"bubble.com"**：dotcom 崩盤前 2~3 個月發出，是他第一篇引起讀者回應的 memo
  - 內容：警告 internet 股票泡沫、blind adoration、FOMO
  - URL：https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo
  - 一段（廣為轉引）：
    > "Even when there's nothing intrinsically wrong... investments can be carried to such heights that you can lose a lot — even when good things happen."

### 2001：方法論基石
- **2001/11/20** — **"You Can't Predict. You Can Prepare."**
  - 標題取自 Mass Mutual 廣告 tagline，Marks 形容為「a Yogi Berra type statement that makes no apparent sense on first reading, but then it turns out to be quite wise」
  - URL: https://www.oaktreecapital.com/docs/default-source/memos/2001-11-20-you-cant-predict-you-can-prepare.pdf
  - 本篇定下他的方法論基調：放棄宏觀預測，改為循環定位

### 2006~2008：金融危機前後三連預警 + 危機底部買入
- **2006/09** — "Dare to Be Great"：定義 second-level thinking 矩陣
- **2007/02/14** — **"The Race to the Bottom"**：金融危機前 1.5 年的預警 memo
  > "Lenders and investors invariably depart from time-honored disciplines when cycles move to extremes, out of a belief that current conditions are different from those that prevailed in the past."
  - URL: https://www.oaktreecapital.com/docs/default-source/memos/2007-02-14-the-race-to-the-bottom.pdf
- **2007/07** — "It's All Good"：第二次預警
- **2008/03** — "The Tide Goes Out"（呼應 Buffett 名言）
- **2008/03** — "The Wisdom of Crowds"
- **2008/10/15** — **"The Limits to Negativism"**：Lehman 倒閉一個月後，他開始買入
  > "It's very important when you're an investor to be a skeptic and not believe everything you hear. And most people think being a skeptic consists of dealing with excessive optimism by saying, 'That's too good to be true.' But when it's pessimism that's excessive, being a skeptic means saying, 'That's too bad to be true.'"
  > "[I am witnessing] the greatest panic I've ever seen, as measured by its severity, the range of assets affected, its worldwide scope and the negativity of the accompanying tales of doom."
  - URL: https://www.oaktreecapital.com/docs/default-source/memos/2008-10-15-the-limits-to-negativism.pdf

### 2014~2017：循環中段提醒
- **2014/09** — "Risk Revisited"
- **2015/03** — "Liquidity"
- **2015/06** — **"Risk Revisited Again"**：定義 risk
  > "What they fear is the possibility of permanent loss. Permanent loss is very different from volatility or fluctuation. A downward fluctuation... isn't a problem if you don't have to sell at that point. ... Permanent loss is very different."
- **2015/09** — "It's Not Easy"：直接挑戰學界的「risk = volatility」
- **2016/01** — "On the Couch"：用心理學解讀市場
- **2016/11** — "Political Reality"：Trump 當選後的論述
- **2017/07/26** — **"There They Go Again... Again"**：再次警告 CAPE 接近歷史第三高
  > "Most people can't think of what might cause trouble anytime soon. But it's precisely when people can't see what it is that could make things turn down that risk is highest, since they tend not to price in risks they can't see."
  > "Some of the memos I'm happiest about having written came at times when bullish trends went too far, risk aversion disappeared and bubbles inflated."

### 2020~2021：疫情、與兒子合著
- **2020/03/03** — "Nobody Knows II"：疫情爆發初期
- **2020/04** — **"Calibrating"**：教如何在環境變化時調倉
  > "Thus, I feel it's a time when previously cautious investors can reduce their overemphasis on defense and begin to move toward a more neutral position or even toward offense."
  > 核心方法：「as the environment becomes more precarious, a portfolio's defensiveness should be increased, and as the environment becomes more propitious, its aggressiveness should be ramped up.」
- **2021/01/11** — **"Something of Value"**：與兒子 Andrew Marks（TQ Ventures 共同創辦人）討論 value vs growth、tech 投資；Marks 自稱這是「我寫過最受歡迎的 memo」

### 2022~2024：Sea Change 時代
- **2022/01** — "Mysterious"
- **2022/07/26** — **"I Beg to Differ"**：定義知識性逆向思維
  > "Unconventional behavior is the only road to superior investment results, but it isn't for everyone. In addition to superior skill, successful investing requires the ability to look wrong for a while and survive some mistakes."
  > **"Most great investments begin in discomfort."**
  - 引用 Joel Greenblatt：「just because no one else will jump in front of a Mack truck barreling down the highway doesn't mean that you should」
- **2022/12** — **"Sea Change"**（重磅）：宣告 40 年低利率時代結束
  > "[A sea change is] 'a complete transformation, a radical change of direction in attitude, goals...'"
  > "In my 53 years in the investment world, I've seen a number of economic cycles, pendulum swings, manias and panics, bubbles and crashes, but I remember only two real sea changes. I think we may be in the midst of a third one today."
  > 主旨：投資環境由 low-return world 轉為 **"full-return world"**——信用工具就能提供穩健回報，不必過度依賴股權/槓桿
  - URL: https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo/sea-change
- **2023/05/30** — **"Further Thoughts on Sea Change"**
  > "There clearly isn't much room for interest rate declines from today's levels, and I don't think short-term interest rates will be as low in the coming years as in the recent past."
  > "I just think they're done coming down."
- **2024/01** — "Ruminating on Asset Allocation"
- **2024/02** — "Easy Money"
- **2024/05** — "Sea Change Rhymes"

### 2025：泡沫觀察
- **2025/01/02** — **"On Bubble Watch"**（bubble.com 25 周年）
  > "The Magnificent Seven are incredible companies, so their high p/e ratios could be warranted, [and] I don't hear people saying, 'there's no price too high;' and the markets, while high-priced and perhaps frothy, don't seem nutty to me."
  - 結論：valuations「lofty but not nutty」——還沒到 2000 年那種程度，但要警惕
- **2025** — "The Folly of Certainty"
- 2022 年起多次以 "What Really Matters?" 為主題重述核心信念

## 四、核心信念（出現 ≥ 3 處）

1. **Risk is not volatility — risk is the permanent loss of capital**
   - 源：*Risk Revisited Again* (2015/06)、《The Most Important Thing》第 5~7 章、CalPERS 演講(2025/01)、多次訪談
   - 為什麼重要：直接挑戰學界 CAPM／beta 框架

2. **Second-level thinking**（看到別人怎麼看 + 我預期市場怎麼反應）
   - 源：《The Most Important Thing》第 1 章、*Dare to Be Great* (2006/09)、*I Beg to Differ* (2022)
   - 招牌示例：
     > "First-level thinking says, 'It's a good company; let's buy the stock.' Second-level thinking says, 'It's a good company, but everyone thinks it's a great company, and it's not. So the stock's overrated and overpriced; let's sell.'"

3. **You can't predict, you can prepare**
   - 源：*You Can't Predict, You Can Prepare* (2001/11)、《Mastering the Market Cycle》、無數訪談
   - 拒絕宏觀預測，轉而做循環定位

4. **It's not what you buy, it's what you pay**
   - 源：Meb Faber Podcast #124（2018/10）即以此為標題；《The Most Important Thing》Ch.4 "The Most Important Thing Is... The Relationship Between Price and Value"
   > "Good investing is not a matter of buying good things, it's a matter of buying things well."

5. **The Pendulum**（市場永遠在 euphoria 與 panic 之間擺盪，極少停在中間）
   - 源：1991 年第二篇 memo、《The Most Important Thing》Ch.9、《Mastering the Market Cycle》整本書骨架

6. **Calibrating aggressiveness vs defensiveness based on cycle position**
   - 源：*Calibrating* (2020/04)、《Mastering the Market Cycle》最終章
   - 核心信條：環境變糟就加防守，環境變好就加攻擊

7. **In good times we leverage, in bad times we deleverage — we should do the opposite**
   - 源：反覆出現於 *Race to the Bottom* (2007)、*Limits to Negativism* (2008/10)、多次訪談
   - 這是他對機構行為週期性犯錯的核心觀察

8. **Most great investments begin in discomfort**
   - 源：*I Beg to Differ* (2022/07)、《The Most Important Thing》Ch.11
   - 配套句：「Unconventional behavior is the only road to superior investment results, but it isn't for everyone.」

9. **Sea Change**——40 年降息週期結束，進入正常化
   - 源：*Sea Change* (2022/12)、*Further Thoughts on Sea Change* (2023/05)、*Sea Change Rhymes* (2024/05)
   - 是他 2020 年後最重要的 macro 論述，跨多篇 memo 與訪談

10. **What the wise man does in the beginning, the fool does in the end**
    - 源：《Mastering the Market Cycle》、多篇 memo
    - 拉丁諺語式定錨，用於警告投資人晚期入場

11. **Skepticism cuts both ways — pessimism when optimism is excessive, optimism when pessimism is excessive**
    - 源：*Limits to Negativism* (2008/10)，全書與訪談反覆出現

## 六、推薦書單 / 智識譜系

- **Ben Graham**（透過 Buffett 與自身學習）——價值投資源頭
- **Warren Buffett**——40+ 年友誼；Buffett 反覆推薦他的 memos 與書，影響極深
- **Charlie Munger**——多次公開稱讚 Munger 的 mental models 框架；Marks 曾說 Munger 對他思維方式的影響「difficult to overstate」
- **Charlie Ellis《Winning the Loser's Game》**——Marks 在《The Most Important Thing》多次援引
- **John Bogle / 指數派**——Marks 雖是主動派，但承認 Bogle 對「平均投資人」的建議是對的
- **Peter Bernstein**——尤其《Against the Gods》，影響 Marks 對 risk 的定義
- **Edward Chancellor《Devil Take the Hindmost》**——Marks 自述讀完此書後對 2000 dotcom bubble 有了結構性理解，直接催生 *bubble.com* memo
- **Nassim Taleb《Fooled by Randomness》**——影響「luck vs skill」框架
- **Daniel Kahneman**——行為金融學影響其 *On the Couch* (2016) 等多篇 memo

## 七、生平骨架（為後續 Agent 提供 timeline 校驗）

- **1946** 出生於紐約皇后區，猶太家庭
- **1967** Wharton 大學部 cum laude，主修 Finance
- **1969** University of Chicago Booth MBA（會計與行銷雙主修，George Hay Brown Prize 得主）
- **1969~1985** Citicorp Investment Management（研究員 → research director → 副總裁，掌管可轉債與高收益債組合）
- **1978** 受 Citi 派去管 high-yield 部門——一個當時被視為「垃圾」的領域，開啟其 distressed / credit 專業
- **1985~1995** TCW Group——CIO of domestic fixed income
- **1995** 與 Bruce Karsh 等 TCW 同事出走，創辦 **Oaktree Capital Management**
- **2012** Oaktree IPO 上市
- **2019** Brookfield Asset Management 收購 Oaktree 62% 股權
- **1990/10/12** 寫下第一篇 memo，至今 35 年未中斷

## Agent 1 摘要（≤200 字）

Howard Marks 是「寫作型」投資家——他的表達結構由 35 年 200+ 篇 Oaktree memos、2 本書、近年與兒子合作的 podcast 三條軸構成。他的方法論基石是「Risk ≠ volatility, risk = permanent loss of capital」與「Second-level thinking」，方法論口號是「You can't predict, you can prepare」。1990 開寫沉默十年，直到 2000/01 *bubble.com* 一戰成名；2007 *Race to the Bottom*、2008/10 *Limits to Negativism* 連續預警金融危機並在底部買入；2022/12 *Sea Change* 宣告 40 年低利率時代結束，2025/01 *On Bubble Watch* 對 AI 泡沫給出「lofty but not nutty」的精準刻度。招牌術語：**Sea Change、The Pendulum、Calibrating、Second-level thinking、I Beg to Differ、What the wise man does in the beginning, the fool does in the end**。智識譜系：Buffett、Munger、Graham、Peter Bernstein、Edward Chancellor、Charlie Ellis。
